# Solution Requirements

## 1. Business Requirements
### BR-001 — Supplier Performance Monitoring
The organization needs to monitor supplier performance across delivery, fulfillment, and procurement measures to identify recurring operational issues.

### BR-002 — Supplier Exposure
The organization needs to understand its dependency on individual suppliers and identify areas where supplier concentration could create operational or financial exposure.

### BR-003 — Risk Indicator Monitoring
The organization needs to identify supplier-related risk indicators and understand which areas of supplier performance may require attention.

### BR-004 - Risk Indicator Status Assessment
The organization needs to know whether supplier risk indicators are within acceptable levels.

### BR-005 — ESG Context
The organization needs to consider selected environmental, social, and governance indicators as additional context when evaluating supplier performance and risk.

## 3. Data Requirements
### DR-001 — Supplier Information
Information identifying and describing suppliers, such as supplier ID, supplier name, location, category, or other relevant attributes.

### DR-002 — Transaction / Order Information
Information describing purchases or orders, including order identifiers, order dates, quantities, values, and associated suppliers and products.

### DR-003 — Delivery Information
Information required to evaluate delivery performance, including expected and actual delivery dates and relevant delivery status or delay information.

### DR-004 — Product Information
Information describing products or materials associated with suppliers and orders, including product identifiers, categories, quantities, and other relevant attributes.

### DR-005 — Procurement Performance Information
Information describing order fulfillment and procurement outcomes, such as PO status, invoice matching, payment status, savings, and other relevant procurement indicators where available.

### DR-006 — ESG Information
Selected environmental, social, and governance attributes associated with suppliers or their operations, where suitable public data is available.
